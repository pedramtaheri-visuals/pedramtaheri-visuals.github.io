Selected Works

A collection of contemporary oil paintings exploring internal environments, quiet domestic architectures, and the subtle light across urban and natural landscapes.

Silent Interior

Oil on Canvas
60 x 50 cm | 2024

An exploration of light falling across empty space, emphasizing atmospheric tension and silence within private chambers.

Suburban Dusk

Oil on Linen Mounted Panel
40 x 40 cm | 2025

Captured entirely outdoors (plein air), studying the transient golden hour colors at the edges of the city.

Studio Light

Oil on Board
70 x 50 cm | 2024

A careful analysis of form and tone under controlled north light within the tradition of academic realism.

The Corner Window

Oil on Canvas
80 x 60 cm | 2023

Focusing on architectural boundaries and the subtle interaction between indoor shadow and external ambient daylight.
