Exhibitions & CV

Born in September 1989 | Based in Iran

Pedram Mohammad Taheri (Pedram M.Taheri) is a contemporary fine artist, professional draughtsman, art educator, and award-nominated nightscape photographer based in Iran. His practice bridges academic drawing traditions with immersive dark-sky explorations.
