Educational Programs

Professional art courses bridging classical drawing traditions with modern structural anatomy. Tailored for independent artists, students, and fine art enthusiasts.

Structural Figure Drawing

A comprehensive program focusing on human anatomy, dynamic structural form, planes of the body, and spatial analysis. Inspired by the strict academic lineage of classical drafting, this course teaches students how to see and reconstruct the human figure with deep volumetric understanding.

  • Understanding skeletal landmarks and core muscle masses.
  • Analysing the figure through volumetric rendering and geometric perspective.
  • Live demonstrations, portfolio reviews, and personalized critiques.

Plein Air Landscape Painting

Immersive outdoor painting sessions studying transient sunlight, atmospheric perspective, and color harmony directly from nature. Students learn the methodology of the 20th-century British realism school, controlling values and capturing the genuine spirit of edge-of-city landscapes.

  • Mastering limited palettes and fast-drying oil techniques.
  • Capturing accurate light values and weather atmospheres.
  • Composition tactics for selecting powerful natural frames.
