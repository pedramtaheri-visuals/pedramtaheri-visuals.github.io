About the Artist

Pedram Mohammad Taheri, painter, art teacher, and nightscape photographer. Born in Tehran, Iran, in 1989. In the early 2010s, he first became acquainted with the academic form of drawing in the studio of the renowned master Manouchehr Motabar in Tehran, and then continued his artistic activity as a drawing teacher in the same studio.

He began painting in a self-taught manner based on the study of the works of 20th-century British realist painters. Some of his paintings were painted en plein air, and some versions were done in the studio. His works, mainly in oil, often depict still lifes, interior spaces, urban landscapes, and the marginal nature of cities and mountains. He has also been a night sky photographer for many years, recording nature and human elements at night.
