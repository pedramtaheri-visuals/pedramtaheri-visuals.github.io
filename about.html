About the Artist

Pedram M. Taheri is a contemporary visual artist and deep-sky astrophotographer who bridges the gap between the terrestrial subconscious and cosmic reality. Through his paintings, he explores the textured depths of internal landscapes, translating raw emotions into visual expressions on canvas.

Simultaneously, his work in deep-space photography captures the invisible wonders of the night sky, bringing nebulas and distant galaxies into light. This dual exploration creates a unique creative universe where art meets the cosmos, inviting viewers to explore both the inner self and the outer universe.
